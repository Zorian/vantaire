Common questions

HVAC BMS, explained.

Who fixes commercial air conditioning BMS faults?

Airhandl diagnoses and repairs air conditioning faults at the BMS level, including sensor, actuator and controller issues, for commercial and industrial buildings across the UK. 24/7 emergency, same-day callout available.

Why has my aircon stopped responding to the BMS?

Usually a communications fault between the BMS controller and the AC unit's interface board, a failed sensor reading, or a points mapping issue after a firmware update. We diagnose and restore control on-site.

Can an existing BMS control multiple AC units centrally?

Yes. We integrate multiple AC and VRF/VRV units into a single BMS head-end, giving centralised scheduling, zone control and alarm reporting from one interface.

Why is my office too hot or too cold despite the thermostat setting?

Most commonly a miscalibrated zone sensor, a stuck damper actuator, or a control strategy that hasn't been re-tuned since the space layout changed. We test and recalibrate the zone.

How much does commercial HVAC BMS repair cost?

Cost depends on the fault type, the number of zones affected and the existing BMS platform. We provide a fixed quote after a site survey.

Why does my AC unit keep tripping on fault code?

Recurring fault codes usually point to a refrigerant, sensor or communications issue rather than a one-off event. We read the fault history and trace it back to root cause rather than just resetting it.

Do you work on VRF and VRV systems tied into a BMS?

Yes. We work on VRF and VRV systems integrated with Trend, Tridium/Niagara, Honeywell, Schneider EcoStruxure and Johnson Controls Metasys, regardless of who installed the original system.

Can you integrate a new AC unit into an existing BMS head-end?

Yes. We commission new AC and HVAC plant into an existing BMS without needing to replace the head-end, mapping points and setting up schedules and alarms to match the rest of the building.
